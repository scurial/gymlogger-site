HOW IT WORKS

Do less. Log more.

Set up your gym once. From then on, showing up is the only action required.

01

Save your gym

Find your Jiu Jitsu gym and set a small boundary around it.

02

Go train

GymLogger notices when you arrive and leave. Your route is never recorded.

03

Find it in Health

Your session appears as a Martial Arts workout, ready for the rest of your fitness history.

BEYOND THE LOG

Watch consistency become progress.

Apple Health, automatically

Qualifying visits become Martial Arts workouts with duration and estimated active energy.

See every week on the mat

Trends that mean something

Friendly leaderboards

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Location-aware.
Not location-tracking.

GymLogger watches only the small circles around gyms you save. It does not record your route, follow you through the day, or send Apple Health data to the cloud.
